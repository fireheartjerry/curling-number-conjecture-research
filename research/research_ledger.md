# Curling Number Conjecture research ledger

This is the canonical chronological lab notebook for this project.  It records
material decisions, proved deductions, computational evidence, falsified
claims, literature checks, and open obligations.  Detailed arguments live in
the linked notes; this ledger records exactly what may and may not be reused.

## Status vocabulary

* **PROVED-NL**: a complete natural-language proof is recorded in the linked
  note and has survived the stated audits.  This is not yet `VERIFIED` for the
  final report because no Lean 4 proof has been supplied.
* **EXTERNAL**: imported from a cited source, with the exact statement and
  applicability recorded in `literature_search_log.md` or the linked note.
* **CHECKED**: executed finite evidence only.  The enumeration domain and
  command must be stated.  It may not support an unbounded conclusion.
* **CONJECTURAL**: an unproved statement or proposed mechanism.
* **REFUTED**: false as stated; a concrete counterexample or countermodel is
  linked.  No later argument may use it without materially stronger hypotheses.
* **BLOCKED**: the route currently ends at an explicitly named missing lemma.

The final unrestricted theorem is not established until every load-bearing
claim is formalized in Lean 4 and the assembled proof passes an independent
adversarial audit.  A numerical curling value is reusable only when produced by
executed code after the A094004 calibration.

## Canonical records

* `research_ledger.md` — chronology, decisions, and admissible reuse.
* `approach_registry.md` — one row per mathematical approach family and its
  exact remaining gap.
* `literature_search_log.md` — queries, sources found, imported statements, and
  negative search results.  A negative search is never a priority claim.
* `dependency_dag.md` and `dependency_graph.py` — proof dependencies.
* Topic notes — full hypotheses, arguments, countermodels, and reproduction
  commands.
* `tests/` and `research/check_*.py` — executable checks.  Their output is

Entry identifiers are immutable and globally unique.  Legacy entries use a
three-digit sequence.  New parallel entries use
`K-YYYYMMDD-HHMM-agent`, where `K` is the status kind and `agent` is a short
task name.  Before appending, an agent must search the ledger for the proposed
identifier.  References to an older entry reuse its identifier but do not
create a new heading with it.

## Standing decisions

### D-001 — Finish line (2026-07-23)

**Decision.** The sole objective is a publication-grade proof that every finite
nonempty integer sequence, over an unbounded integer alphabet, reaches curling
number one.  Bounded alphabets, bounded lengths, density statements, heuristic
termination, and reductions to unproved statements are not substitutes.

**Consequence.** No partial route may be presented as a solution.  A surviving
natural-language proof must then be translated to Lean 4 and independently
audited before any journal claim.

### D-002 — Evidence firewall (2026-07-23)

**Decision.** Natural-language proofs, finite computations, external theorems,
and conjectures are distinct evidence classes.  They are never silently
upgraded.  Every maximizing factorization must prove maximality; a displayed
power gives only a lower bound.

**Consequence.** Every numerical curling number or orbit transition in a note
must be generated by executed code.  Calibration convention: OEIS A094004 is
total orbit length including the start, so the required checks include
`a(3)=5`, `a(8)=66`, and `a(22)=142`.

### D-003 — Literature-first, novelty-second (2026-07-23)

**Decision.** Before investing in a lemma, search primary literature for the
same or a stronger result and import it when available.  Then continue with new
project-specific mathematics.  The project may say only “not found in the
searched literature,” never claim priority from a search failure.

### D-004 — Failure preservation (2026-07-23)

**Decision.** Failed lemmas and finite countermodels remain first-class
artifacts.  They are recorded with the missing hypothesis so later rounds do
not rediscover and reuse them.

### D-005 — Current convergence target (2026-07-23)

**Decision.** The active structural target is the contexted critical-cube
promotion problem for primitive circular fixed profiles.  Restart-cycle,
maximum-square, and singleton-`3` gadget analyses are being reduced toward that
same target while incompatible general-label and endpoint-rank routes remain
alive.

### D-007 — Parallel provenance identifiers (2026-07-23)

**Decision.** Parallel agents may not allocate bare sequential IDs.  They use
the timestamp-and-task identifier convention above.  A ledger audit found and
renumbered three collisions: the cap-ascent proof is `P-010`, the Q21
boundary-mask computation is `C-006`, and the small whole-cycle enumeration is
`C-007`.  No mathematical content changed.

### D-008 — No unlogged research state (2026-07-24)

**Decision.** A material proof search action is not reusable until it has a
ledger entry.  Each entry must identify the exact hypotheses, outcome,
evidence class, dependencies, scope limitation, linked artifact, and (for
code) the calibrated reproduction command.  Refutations must preserve the
counterexample and name the missing hypothesis.  Agent handoffs must update
the approach registry when they change the strongest known result or exact
bottleneck.

**Consequence.** Chat memory and agent summaries are navigation aids only;
they are never mathematical provenance.  Before starting a branch, search the
ledger, registry, and literature log.  Before reusing a result, read its linked
proof or checker and verify that its status supports the intended inference.
This rule is specifically intended to prevent duplicated work, hypothesis
drift, and accidental promotion of computations or attractive reductions into
proofs.

**PROVED-NL P-20260724-0001-RCSC-2 — Locked-versus-external prefix
classification.**  Let `Q=Rx` be primitive of length `n>=3`, put

```
V=Q^3 H,        U=x Q^3 H.
```

`U=Y^k`, `cn(V)=k-1`.  For `r=|Y|` and `g=gcd(n,r)`, Fine--Wilf gives the

